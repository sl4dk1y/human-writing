I kept a notebook for eleven years and then stopped. Not for any good reason. I think I got tired of my own handwriting.

What I remember from those notebooks is mostly the boring entries: "bought stamps", "call the dentist back", the phone number of a man who fixed the boiler in 2016 and never picked up again. The ideas I thought were too important to lose are gone. I never once went back to look at them.

There is one page I still think about. It says, in bad Russian, "the dog knows something". I have no idea what that meant. My wife says I wrote it after the neighbour's dog died, but I don't believe her, and anyway the notebook doesn't say.
